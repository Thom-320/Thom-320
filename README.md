I'm an undergraduate interested in how people and artificial agents learn,
coordinate, and make decisions. Here you'll find my work on recurrent networks,
collective search, and reinforcement learning, alongside systems programming
and simulation projects.

My interests span **computational neuroscience, NeuroAI, reinforcement learning,
and reliable AI**. I like building models, testing their assumptions, and figuring
out why a promising result changes under better controls.

## How I work

I use held-out evaluation, automated tests, and reproducible experiments to
check what a model actually establishes. In SCRES, giving a stronger static
policy the same decision options as PPO overturned the apparent learning
advantage in that comparison. The
[experiment and verdict](https://github.com/Thom-320/scres-ia/blob/main/docs/TRACK_B_SAME_CONTRACT_CHALLENGE_VERDICT_2026-07-10.md)
are public, including the result that did not support the original claim.
