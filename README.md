# Thomas Chisica

Undergraduate in **Applied Mathematics and Computer Science** at **Universidad del Rosario** (Bogota, Colombia), supported by a national merit scholarship. I am building toward a research career in **NeuroAI** and **computational neuroscience**, especially at the intersection of active inference, reinforcement learning, graph theory, and collective behavior.

My public work sits between mathematics, machine learning, and systems for studying how agents, biological or artificial, coordinate and adapt under uncertainty. I care about reproducible artifacts: explicit assumptions, runnable pipelines, visible evidence, and honest limits.

## Research

- **Collective behavior and multi-agent coordination** with Prof. Edgar Andrade-Lotero: spectral and graph-theoretic analysis of self-organized division of cognitive labor, connected to broader PsyNet-based behavioral experimentation.
- **Supply-chain resilience as a learned capability** with Prof. Alexander Garrido: reinforcement learning over discrete-event simulations of disruption; manuscript in preparation.
- **NeuroAI preparation**: incoming participant in Neuromatch Academy 2026, NeuroAI track.

I keep collaborative or private research material out of public repositories unless it has been cleared for release. The projects below are the public artifacts I currently use as my research and software portfolio.

## Working Principles

- Make public claims traceable to code, figures, tests, or documented experiments.
- Separate research artifacts from private or uncleared collaborative material.
- Prefer small reproducible pipelines over large demos that cannot be inspected.
- State limitations directly, especially when a repository is educational, exploratory, or still moving toward publication.
